# Code of conduct

This is a small project with one maintainer. The short version, I want people to
be able to file a bug, ask a question, or disagree with a decision without it
costing them anything.

## What that means in practice

Be straight with people. Disagreeing with an idea is fine and useful,
belittling the person who had it is not. Assume the person you are replying to
is competent and short of time, because they usually are.

If someone asks something the README already answers, point them at it kindly.
Everyone has been the person who missed the obvious paragraph.

Not welcome, and it is a short list. Personal attacks, harassment of any kind,
sexual attention, publishing anyone's private information, and sustained
disruption of a thread after being asked to stop.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately through GitHub, or by opening a security advisory if you
would rather it stayed unlisted while it is dealt with. I will look at what
happened and act on it, which may mean editing or removing comments, or blocking
someone from the repository. I will tell you what I did.

If the problem is with something I said or did, say so. I would rather hear it
than not.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.
